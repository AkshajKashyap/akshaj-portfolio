# Featured Project Review

Updated September 13, 2026. The shipped hierarchy is intentionally fixed at four projects:

1. **CUDA Transformer Runtime, lead.** Makes GPU inference and performance depth visible while retaining correctness evidence and avoiding production-serving claims.
2. **Causal Uplift Experimentation, major.** Keeps rigorous experimentation central, leads with methodology and measured lift, labels the trial simulation as synthetic, and records the real-validation hold.
3. **Molecular Property Prediction with GNNs, standard.** Demonstrates graph learning, scaffold-aware evaluation, repeated seeds, and scientific application context.
4. **BlockScope, standard.** Adds protocol-level execution analysis, historical EVM replay, and carefully bounded counterfactual evidence without implying intent, wallet loss, or realized profit.

The combination presents an AI/ML-oriented software engineer with credible depth across modeling, inference, experimentation, scientific software, and reliable execution. CUDA receives the existing lead slot and remains the only featured project with a visual.

Homepage additional work is limited to C++ Matching Engine, Gridiron Spatial Intelligence, Plant Disease Classification, and LLM Posttraining Ops, in that order. MatchStream is the first archive-only project. The homepage selection favors complementary technical signal; it does not imply that LLM Posttraining has stronger standalone evidence than MatchStream.

Graph Kernel SVM remains in structured data but is not rendered because its public repository does not contain the reports needed to inspect the previously published macro-F1 claims.

## Claim controls

- CUDA numbers are tied to the repository's RTX 3050 and CPU-reference evidence; no engine comparison or production claim is made.
- Causal lift is explicitly a synthetic prospective-trial simulation, and promotion remains on hold pending real randomized validation.
- Molecular GNN metrics identify the ESOL scaffold split, three seeds, and the comparison baseline.
- BlockScope is described as a `v0.1.0` alpha tag, not a GitHub release. The small eight-block corpus is not treated as representative, and observed replay/counterfactual differences are not described as confirmed attacks, intent, wallet loss, or realized profit.
- Matching Engine performance is tied to one WSL2/ext4 host, 16 clients, and the same-run cap-1 control.
- Gridiron uses checksum-backed aggregate evidence; full reproduction requires separately authorized NFL competition data.
- LLM Posttraining's tiny fixtures and one-step SFT/DPO validate infrastructure wiring, not model-quality improvement.
- MatchStream retains its local 3,549-event replay and throughput evidence in the archive.
- Valorant copy calls its 2024 evidence modest and the advantage unstable.
